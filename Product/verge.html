Verge

Minimal everyday carry wallet (EDC)

Verge is a compact, minimalist EDC wallet precision CNC-milled from a solid billet. Instead of hiding the cards, its integrated clamping mechanism secures a partial stack to celebrate their design, eliminating visual noise while reducing material and sensory weight. Refined through iterative CAD and hands-on prototyping, the final geometry ensures a perfectly clean fit and a crisp, tactile feel.

Category Millwork

Date 2025

Duration 5 Weeks

Skills CNC Machining, Fusion 360, Keyshot

Objective

Developing a precision-milled EDC wallet that reduces visual and physical weight through an integrated clamping mechanism.

Iterative Prototyping

The final geometry was refined through multiple CAD iterations and 3D-printed samples to tune the clamping force and card accessibility. CNC toolpaths were optimized to maintain crisp edges while ensuring a smooth surface finish directly off the mill.
