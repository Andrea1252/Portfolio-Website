Lululemon Weeding Fork

Brand identity exploration

A design exercise exploring how Lululemon’s lifestyle-focused brand identity could extend beyond apparel into gardening tools. This project reimagines the humble weeding fork as a premium, ergonomic product that reflects the brand’s sleek, performance-driven aesthetic and wellness ethos.

Category Product

Date 2023

Duration 4 Weeks

Skills Brand Extension, Prototyping, Solidworks

Objective

Reimagining a traditional gardening tool through Lululemon’s design language to prioritize ergonomics and premium performance.

Brand Identity & Form Study

Exploration of Lululemon's visual identity—fluid lines, high-performance materials, and human-centric ergonomics—translated into a physical tool. The form was developed using Solidworks to ensure precision in the ergonomic transitions and curvature.
